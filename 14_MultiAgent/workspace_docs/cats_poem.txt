Whiskers and Shadow
===================

Soft paws on the floor,
A shadow that slips through doors,
Silent, sleek, and sure.

Eyes like lantern glow,
Watching worlds that mortals miss,
Ancient, wise, and still.

Curled beside the sun,
Dreaming of birds yet to chase,
Purring like a stream.

Mysterious friend,
You need no one, yet you stay —
Grace in fur and whim.
