Whiskers in the Shadows

They move like liquid moonlight,
Through the quiet, darkened hall,
With velvet paws that touch the earth
And make no sound at all.

Their eyes are burning lanterns,
A gold and emerald flame,
They stare into the spaces
Where the wild things still remain.

They stretch like lazy rivers
In the warmth of afternoon,
Then curl into a crescent—
A reflection of the moon.

They carry ancient secrets
From the temples where they reigned,
In every graceful gesture,
That royal blood remained.

Oh, creatures of the twilight,
Half shadow and half dream,
More mystery than morning,
More magic than they seem.
